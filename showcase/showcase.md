# Shatter

Shatter is a fast top-down rock shooter played on a single wrapping field. Fly a
small ship through deep space, keep your momentum under control, and clear each
wave by breaking every armored rock down through its fragments.

The star at the centre is the trick. It pulls rocks and bullets into curved
paths, but leaves the ship alone. A shot can bend around the well and arrive at
a rock from the far side; a rock that reaches the core is recycled back in from
the edge, so the field never stops moving.

Larger rocks absorb several gun hits before splitting. A saucer eventually hunts
the field and fires at your current position. Your Warhead torpedo is one guided
munition on a ten-second recharge: it flies straight through the well and
destroys any rock outright when it finds one in its narrow forward cone.
